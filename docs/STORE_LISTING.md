**Full description (≤4000 chars):**

Know your skin. Fix what matters. See real progress.

ClearSkin AI is your personal skin coach — built for every skin tone, every gender and every budget.

📸 AI SKIN SCAN IN 10 SECONDS
Take a guided selfie and get your skin score across 9 areas: blemishes, texture, pores, redness, even tone, hydration, oil balance, smoothness and under-eyes. Our guided camera checks your lighting and angle so every scan is comparable — that's how you see real change, not lighting tricks.

🧴 A ROUTINE THAT FITS YOU
Morning and evening steps based on mainstream dermatology guidance — not ads. Your actives are scheduled on different nights so they never clash. Pregnant? Sensitive skin? Deeper skin tone? Shave daily? Your plan adapts.

🔍 SCAN ANY PRODUCT
Scan a barcode or snap the ingredient list. See instantly if a product suits YOUR skin: pore-clogging ingredients for acne-prone skin, fragrance for sensitive skin, ingredients to pause during pregnancy — and what's genuinely good for you. No fear-mongering about safe ingredients.

⚠️ ROUTINE CLASH CHECKER
Retinol with acids on the same night? Actives without sunscreen? ClearSkin flags combinations that cause irritation and tells you how to fix them.

📈 TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Score trends, a private photo timeline, and before-and-after comparisons you can share.

🧠 FIND YOUR TRIGGERS
A 10-second daily check-in (sleep, stress, food, workouts, your cycle) reveals what's behind YOUR breakouts.

☀️ DAILY SKIN FORECAST
UV, humidity and air quality for your city, turned into simple advice for today.

💬 AI SKIN COACH
Ask anything. Answers are based on your skin, your routine and the products you own.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos stay on your phone. Location data is stripped. Optional fingerprint/face lock. Export or delete everything anytime. We never sell your data.

ClearSkin AI provides cosmetic guidance and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. For changing moles, painful or scarring acne, or any concern about your health, see a doctor or dermatologist.

Premium unlocks unlimited scans, progress comparisons, trigger insights, the AI coach and unlimited product checks. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Google Play at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
