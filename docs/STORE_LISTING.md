**Full description (≤4000 chars):**

```
Know your skin. Fix what matters. See real progress.

ClearSkin AI measures your skin from a guided face scan, builds a routine around it and shows you what is actually changing. Made for every skin tone and every gender.

A skin scan that measures
Take a front photo and two side views. The camera waits for good light and a clear view, so every scan is comparable. You get a skin score across nine areas: blemishes, texture, pores, redness, even tone, hydration, oil balance, smoothness and under-eyes, plus a face map that shows where each finding is.

A routine built around your skin
Morning and evening steps based on mainstream dermatology guidance. Actives are spaced so they never clash. Pregnant, sensitive, deeper skin tone or shaving daily? Your plan adapts. Every step tells you how much to use, how long to leave it and when to stop.

A 12-week plan with a finish line
Reset, treat, refine. See where you started, where you are and where you are heading.

Know if a product suits you
Scan a barcode or the ingredient list. See what suits your skin, what to pause during pregnancy and which products clash with each other.

Find what sets off your breakouts
A 10-second daily check-in on sleep, stress, food, workouts and your cycle shows what lines up with your breakouts.

See real progress
Score trends, a private photo timeline and before-and-after comparisons.

Ask the skin coach
Questions answered from your scans, your routine and the products you own.

Daily skin forecast
UV, humidity and air quality for your city, turned into one line of advice for today.

Private by design
Your photos are stored on your phone. Photos sent for analysis are never stored on our servers, sold or shown to advertisers, and never used to train AI. Location data is stripped. Optional fingerprint or face lock. Export or delete everything at any time.

Free and Premium
Free: 2 AI skin scans every 30 days, your routine, reminders and streaks, with ads. Watch an optional video for one extra scan.
Premium: unlimited scans (fair use), the skin coach, full progress history and comparisons, breakout-trigger insights, unlimited product checks and no ads.
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Google Play at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Prepaid plans do not renew.

ClearSkin AI gives cosmetic guidance and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. For changing moles, painful or scarring acne, or any concern about your health, see a doctor or dermatologist.
```
